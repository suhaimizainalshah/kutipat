# Kutipat privacy policy

**Kutipat collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.** There are no analytics, no counters and no tracking, and Kutipat keeps no record of the sites you visit.

## What is stored
- **On your device only:** your saved setups (for each site: where its data is, your column names and choices, and your More pages setting). Remove a site’s setup by unticking **Remember this setup**, or remove them all by uninstalling Kutipat.
- **Collected data** stays in the side panel until you export it, and is not stored.
- **For this browser session only:** the address of a page where you clicked Kutipat’s toolbar button, kept in memory so Kutipat can offer access to that site. It is cleared when the browser closes.

## Network requests
Kutipat makes none, except reading a site’s own `robots.txt` file when you start collecting more pages from that site, to tell you whether the site asks automated tools not to visit.

## Permissions
- **activeTab and scripting:** to read the page you are on, only when you open Kutipat on it.
- **sidePanel:** to show Kutipat beside the page.
- **storage:** to keep your saved setups on your device.
- **Access to a site (optional):** requested only when you collect more pages by following a Next button onto new pages, and only for that one site. You can remove it at any time in Chrome’s extension settings.
